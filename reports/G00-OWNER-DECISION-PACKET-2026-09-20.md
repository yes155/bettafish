# G00 Owner Decision Packet — BettaFish.website

Date: 2026-09-20  
Status: **Owner confirmation required**

The project already has a fixed domain, editorial scope, safety boundaries, publisher identity, contact channel and delivery workflow. G00 only needs the business/audience decisions below.

## Proposed defaults

### Audience

**English-speaking betta owners, with US-first search research and both US and metric units where measurements matter.**

Why this fits the current build:
- source/query research is US-first;
- the site already uses `en-US`;
- the content is useful internationally and should not pretend to be US-only.

### Primary outcome

**Build a trusted organic-search authority resource for betta care, health, behavior, biology and aquarium decisions.**

This matches the current information architecture and trust/review model.

### Monetization stance

**Launch editorially independent and without monetization claims. Advertising and/or affiliate monetization may be added later only with clear disclosure, without changing factual recommendations or reviewer independence.**

This keeps the launch clean while preserving future options.

## Proposed 30 / 90 / 180-day success framework

These are operational SEO targets, not traffic or revenue guarantees.

### 30 days after launch

- Google Search Console and analytics collecting data;
- production sitemap accepted;
- no sitewide accidental noindex/canonical/robots errors;
- all 54 launch URLs discoverable through sitemap and internal links;
- no material coverage/security/manual-action issue requiring rollback;
- establish baseline impressions, queries and indexed-page count.

### 90 days after launch

- non-brand impressions visible across multiple core clusters rather than only the homepage;
- no persistent duplicate/canonical/crawl errors affecting a content cluster;
- at least 25% of launch articles have received organic impressions;
- identify the first pages needing title/CTR, internal-link or content-refresh work from real Search Console data;
- no new content cluster is added solely because of anecdotal keyword ideas.

### 180 days after launch

- at least 50% of launch articles have received organic impressions;
- sustained non-brand organic clicks exist across more than one topical cluster;
- content-refresh priorities are based on query/page evidence rather than blanket rewriting;
- monetization decision is revisited only after traffic and audience mix are measurable;
- editorial, clinical-review and corrections processes remain intact.

## Explicitly not used as launch-success promises

Do not set arbitrary:
- traffic guarantees;
- ranking-position guarantees;
- revenue guarantees;
- backlink quotas;
- “X pages must rank top 10” promises.

Those depend on external systems and would turn the charter into an outcome promise rather than an operating framework.
