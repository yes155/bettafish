# PAA Care FAQ Expansion Proposal

Date: 2026-09-19  
Branch: `chatgpt-work`  
Status: **Proposal only — no approved source changes**

## Trigger from the PAA file

The PAA export repeatedly surfaces two supporting task families that the site can answer without new URLs:

### Handling / touching / bites
- Why can't you touch betta fish?
- Can a betta fish bite hurt?
- Will a betta fish bite your finger?

### Feeding detail
- how often should a betta be fed;
- how do you tell if a betta is hungry;
- fixed pellet-count questions;
- can bettas eat human food;
- what foods are harmful/toxic;
- live-food questions;
- fasting/absence questions.

## Decision

**Do not create standalone pages.**

Use:
- `/care/tank-setup/` for handling/contact boundaries;
- `/care/diet-feeding/` for feeding-detail variants.

## Proposed Tank Setup FAQ additions

### FAQ: Can you touch a betta fish?

Answer direction:
- routine touching is unnecessary;
- a fish's protective mucus/skin and delicate fins should not be treated like a pettable surface;
- use a container or fish-safe transfer method where practical rather than handling by hand;
- avoid chasing or squeezing;
- do not turn the answer into human-injury advice.

Editorial review required.

### FAQ: Do betta fish bite people?

Answer direction:
- a betta may nip at a finger or food-associated target;
- this is not a reason to provoke biting as enrichment;
- keep the page focused on safe handling and aquarium care;
- avoid medical claims about wound risk unless sourced and reviewed.

Editorial review required.

## Proposed Diet FAQ additions

### FAQ: How can I tell if my betta is hungry?

Answer direction:
- eagerness at feeding time does not prove underfeeding;
- use a measured feeding routine, body condition, leftovers and product directions;
- do not increase food solely because the fish begs at the glass.

### FAQ: How many pellets should I feed?

The current article already rejects one universal pellet count.

Use an exact-query FAQ to reinforce:
- pellet sizes and calorie density differ;
- follow the product label and observe the fish;
- avoid a sitewide fixed number.

### FAQ: Can bettas eat human food?

Answer direction:
- routine diet should be a complete species-appropriate fish food;
- do not recommend kitchen foods as staples;
- avoid a long unsourced “toxic foods” list;
- route accidental ingestion or illness signs to qualified fish-health advice if clinically relevant.

## Cannibalization rule

Do not create:
- `/care/can-you-touch-betta-fish/`
- `/care/do-betta-fish-bite/`
- `/care/how-many-pellets/`
- `/care/betta-human-food/`

These are supporting FAQs within stronger canonical owners.

## Approval boundary

Editorial reapproval is required for visible FAQ additions.

Clinical review is only required if the final text adds:
- injury treatment;
- toxicity diagnosis;
- medication;
- emergency thresholds;
- disease-specific feeding treatment.
