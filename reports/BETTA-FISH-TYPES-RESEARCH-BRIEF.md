## Page job

Explain that **“betta type” is not one scientific category**.

A domestic betta can be described across several independent axes:
1. tail/fin form;
2. color and pattern;
3. body/size form;
4. sex;
5. true biological species.

The page should help readers classify a fish without implying that hobby labels are separate species.

## Unique promise
