### 3. “Are female bettas smaller?”

Do not invent a universal sex-size rule.

Answer direction:

- actual size queries belong to Growth/Size;
- use multiple traits rather than body size alone for sex identification.

Clarify:

- “female” is a sex category, not a separate color/tail species type;
- route “types” to the new Betta Fish Types overview after approval.
