# Velvet and Columnaris Clinical Controlled Batch

**Problem:** The supplied drafts prescribed fixed copper, blackout, cooling and dual-antibiotic protocols and presented visual signs as diagnoses.

**Severity:** Critical pet-health risk. Copper safety depends on formulation and water chemistry, while inappropriate antimicrobial use can harm the fish, biofilter and future treatment options.

**Affected URLs:** `/diseases/velvet-treatment/` and `/diseases/columnaris-treatment/`.

**Correction:** Both sources are now symptom-first clinical-review candidates. Velvet content requires microscopic confirmation and case-specific copper/product planning. Columnaris content emphasizes wet mount, culture, susceptibility testing, environmental correction and urgent escalation. Universal dosing and temperature recipes were removed.

**Regression risk controlled:** Exact source hashes are recorded in the clinical-candidate register. Both pages are non-indexable and must remain absent from generated output until Robert Martinez, DVM, approves those exact versions and media is approved.
