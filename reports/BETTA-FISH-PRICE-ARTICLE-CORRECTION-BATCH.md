# Betta Fish Price Controlled Batch

**Problem:** A permanent price guide can become inaccurate quickly and can make the fish's purchase price overshadow the cost of safe housing and care.

**Severity:** Medium, date-sensitive.

**Affected URL:** `/economics/betta-fish-price/`.

**Correction:** The structured source uses a dated September 2026 retail snapshot only to demonstrate variation. It provides a reproducible first-year budget method covering housing, heat, filtration, tests, consumables, utilities and a health reserve. Retail sources are explicitly not endorsements.

**Regression risk controlled:** The page does not rank vendors, promise prices or add affiliate behavior. It remains blocked pending editorial approval and media.
