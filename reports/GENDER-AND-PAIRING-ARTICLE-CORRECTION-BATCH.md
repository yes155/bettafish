# Gender Identification and Pairing/Fry Care Controlled Batch

**Problem:** The supplied drafts identified sex from one marker and prescribed fixed pairing temperatures, hatch timing and feeding schedules without sufficient welfare boundaries.

**Severity:** High animal-welfare risk because misidentification or an unprepared pairing can cause injury and leave large numbers of fry without suitable care.

**Affected URLs:** `/breeding/gender-identification/` and `/breeding/pairing-fry-care/`.

**Correction:** The identification page now requires multiple mature traits and preserves uncertainty for juveniles and Plakat fish. The breeding page requires permanent adult housing, protected introduction, stop conditions, live-food readiness and grow-out capacity. Controlled-study timing is labeled as evidence, not a guarantee.

**Regression risk controlled:** No new hub was created and no compatibility promise was added. Both pages remain blocked from output pending exact-version editorial approval and media.
